# BERX — PRODUCT CAPABILITY MAP

## Social
Identity, profiles, graph, feed, posts, comments, reactions, saves, sharing, stories, reels, communities, messaging.

## Real world
Places, nearby discovery, NOW, check-ins, events, experiences, trips, memories.

## Discovery
People, creators, places, events, communities, hashtags, trending and personalized discovery.

## Match
Profile discovery, preferences, like/pass/superlike, mutual matches, conversation entry, safety/report/block.

## Rewards
Points, levels, achievements, verified actions, leaderboards and reward history.

## Business
Business profile, claim, team, media, reviews, replies, offers, moments, events, analytics and booking boundary.

## Creator
Creator profile, content, audience, real view log, insights and media.

## Commerce
Offer/product presentation, booking flow, booking management and payment provider boundary. Payment is BLOCKED until a real provider is configured.

## Trust & Safety
Reports, blocks, privacy, circles, moderation, admin audit log and security controls.

## Personalization without AI dependency
BERX does not require AI. Ranking can begin with deterministic rules: relationship strength, recency, proximity, engagement, freshness, quality and explicit preferences. AI can be added later as an optional service without becoming the source of truth.
